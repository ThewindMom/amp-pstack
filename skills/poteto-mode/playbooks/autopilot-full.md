### Autopilot-full

**You own the verdicts, never the PRs. One owner runs each PR from build to merge, and nothing merges without your clean swarm verdict.** For "autopilot this queue", "full autopilot", and one-owner-per-PR programs. The job is a queue of independent PRs handed over to drive to merged with full autonomy. Orchestrate runs a standing program whose coordinator lands verified work itself and whose workers never merge; here each PR's owner carries the whole lifecycle through the merge, and the root keeps only verification, countersigns, and audits.

1. **Mark the operator's items and honor state-then-wait.** Items the operator names stay theirs. They review and click, and no owner merges one. When asked only to state the protocol or plan, deliver it and stop. Execution starts on explicit approval. Store the full objective and done predicate in durable thread state and the decision trail.
2. **Spawn one owner per PR with the full lifecycle and an early trail.** Resolve the forge once for the program. GitHub CLI (`gh`) is the default. If `command -v origin` succeeds and Origin can resolve the repository, use `origin pr ...` for PR create, edit, view, watch, and merge operations; otherwise stay on `gh` and record the fallback. Never require Graphite (`gt`). One Amp orb thread per PR owns build, the first push, a ready PR, self-proof on the real artifact, skeptical Bugbot triage, diff cleanup, **no-comments**, a rebase onto current trunk, babysit to green, and the merge when authorized. Use native `create_thread` when the operator specifies orb size or agent mode. Once shared writes are authorized, within about 15 minutes every owner starts an uncommitted `decisions.tsv` trail, pushes its first branch snapshot, and opens the PR ready, never draft. Open the PR before self-proof so the URL, decisions, and checks form a durable trail. Every owner reports the trail to the root thread. Step 4 gates every merge. Shared pushes and PR writes still require the operator's explicit authorization.
3. **Run owners in true parallel and never stack.** Many owners at once when PRs are self-contained: one writer per branch, disjoint files, cross-PR drift absorbed by rebase. Only genuinely overlapping work serializes. Self-contained PRs branch straight off main, and sequenced work is merge-then-branch. One exception: an owner that must split a genuinely dependent change may hold a short private base-branch stack.
4. **Swarm-verify every merge-ready head before its merge.** At the owner's merge-ready head SHA, fan out parallel independent verifiers per **swarm** and aggregate one verdict. Re-run gates, prove the load-bearing behavior with the repository's real-surface verification skill, and audit receipts and diff without trusting the PR body. One lane is the **Regression lane against trunk.** Run the same load-bearing scenario on current trunk. If trunk does not have the feature, record that fact and gate the behavior the diff adds plus the end state the user waits for instead of pretending trunk can produce it. The live lane is the floor, and a verdict without it is not clean. No merge without the root's clean verdict. Findings go back to the owner for fix-forward, and the new head gets a fresh swarm and a fresh verdict.
5. **On a clean verdict the owner merges and takes the next item.** The owner merges only from a head freshly rebased onto trunk. The merge-ready report is made at a trunk-current head, and the swarm verdict pins that SHA. If trunk moves again before the merge, the patch-id rule in `playbooks/shipping.md` governs re-verification; a new head voids the verdict unless the patch-id is unchanged. The owner squash-merges its own PR through the resolved forge and picks up its next self-contained item from the queue. The operator's explicit full-autonomy grant plus the root's clean verdict is the merge authorization that babysitting alone never has. Operator-named items stop at merge-ready and wait for their click.
6. **Run the root layer.** A new raise of a pinned gate or budget needs a fresh countersign after verifier proof. For a user-requested ongoing run, schedule an audit tick at a meaningful cadence, commonly 30 minutes. Each tick re-reads this playbook and the durable objective, checks Amp child-thread status, collects decision trails, and counts only side effects as progress. Replace a stuck lane rather than waiting indefinitely.
7. **Stand down instantly on the operator's stop.** Her hold or stand-down reaches every owner as a zero-writes order immediately. Owners hold their briefs until she releases them.

**Reply:** the queue with each PR's owner, state, and head SHA; each verdict and the swarm that produced it; what merged and what each owner took next; countersigns granted and why; open operator gates; where the collected decision trails live.
